# MCGA Brand Architecture Refresh

## Goal

Reposition the site as MCGA TECHNOLOGIES LLC's corporate home, with StayLink presented as one product brand among a growing future portfolio.

## Design

- Make MCGA's company mission the sole hero message; remove the product-led hero CTA.
- Increase readable breathing room through calmer body typography, generous section rhythm, and less dense card treatment.
- Reduce corporate copy to short, clear statements about dependable software, privacy, and thoughtful product development.
- Replace the product-heavy capabilities narrative with a dedicated **Brands & products** section. The first card identifies StayLink as an MCGA brand; a quiet companion card reserves space for future brands.
- Keep the dark network-console visual language, responsive navigation, accessibility hooks, and contact footer.

## Validation

Verify the revised brand hierarchy and key content statically, ensure the existing interaction script remains valid, and run the full Node test suite.
